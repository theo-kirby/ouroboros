## Proposed ten-run screening batch

Two fresh runs of each configuration, interleaved in a prospectively frozen order:
1. Current-system control.
2. Explicit publication structure: answer, practical scale, ordinary/failure examples,
   visual evidence, limitations, expandable methods.
3. Dependency-aware verification reuse, invalidated by any changed dependency.
4. Assumption-focused existing critic: metric semantics, leakage, inference boundaries,
   and evidence that could overturn the answer.
5. Evidence-backed completion policy: remaining uncertainty and material value of
   another cycle, with early completion and genuine blockers both tested.

Same task, model, effort, backend and total resource ceilings. These are screening
comparisons, not enough replication for a definitive ranking. Test verification reuse
against corrupt predictions, changed semantics, wrong chart geometry and overclaims
before live admission. Additional agents and model changes are later separate factors.

Record all-role usage, failed attempts, first sufficient answer/verification/publication,
final stop and intervening work. Reader tests use identical questions and counterbalanced
order: winning predictor, meaning of factor 1.50, informative failure, dinosaur boundary,
and locating supporting evidence. Retain raw answers, timing and confidence.
